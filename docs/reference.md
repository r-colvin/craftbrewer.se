## Books

### Essential

**How to Brew Beer in Your Kitchen** — John J. Palmer
The starting point for kitchen-scale brewing. Palmer's distilled guide for 10-litre batches. Primary reading recommendation for BrewClub. See [BrewClub Resources](/homebrewing/brewclub/resources) for chapter references.

**How to Brew** — John J. Palmer, 4th ed. (Brewers Publications, 2017)
The definitive homebrewing reference. Covers extract through all-grain, water chemistry, yeast management, and troubleshooting. The graduate text after *How to Brew Beer in Your Kitchen*. First edition free at [howtobrew.com](https://www.howtobrew.com).
