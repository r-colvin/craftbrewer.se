# Getting started

Before brew day, there are a handful of decisions to make — and they flow in a specific order. Get these right once and they stay right across every brew in BrewClub.

**Batch size** is the root decision. How much beer you're making determines everything else: which fermenter fits, how to split a kit between club members, and how many kegs or bottles you need.

**Fermenter** follows directly from batch size. The available PP bucket sizes are 5.5 L, 15 L, and 30 L — your batch size determines which one is right.

**Packaging** is the next choice: keg or bottles. This decision is independent of batch size but shapes your brew day and how you serve and share the finished beer.

**Carbonation** follows from packaging — how you carbonate depends on what you're packaging into.

**Sanitiser** runs in parallel with all of the above. It doesn't depend on the other decisions, but it needs to be sorted before brew day. BrewClub keeps it simple.

Work through each section in order. By the end you'll have everything confirmed and brew day is just following the steps.
