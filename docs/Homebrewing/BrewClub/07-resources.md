# Resources and further reading

BrewClub is a starting point, not a finishing line. Once you've brewed a batch or two, these resources will help you go further — understanding the science behind what you're doing, exploring new styles and techniques, and connecting with the wider brewing community.

---

## Books

### How to Brew — John Palmer

The textbook for home brewing. Palmer covers everything from extract brewing through to all-grain, with clear explanations of the science behind each step. It is the most widely recommended starting point in the hobby and the primary reference that has shaped much of the thinking behind BrewClub.

The full fourth edition is available free online at [howtobrew.com](https://www.howtobrew.com). If you find yourself using it regularly, the paper copy is worth having.
