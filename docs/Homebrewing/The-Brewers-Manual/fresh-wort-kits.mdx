## What is a Fresh Wort Kit?

A Fresh Wort Kit (FWK) is professionally brewed, fully hopped wort — packaged in a sealed bag-in-box and ready to ferment at home. No mash, no boil, no pot required.

## Volume maths — the key constraint

FWKs from olbryggning.se come as **15L of wort concentrate**. The standard dilution is 3 parts wort to 1 part water, giving 20L at standard ABV. You do not have to use all 15L at once.

### Headspace rules

Every fermenter needs headspace for CO2 release and krausen during active fermentation.

| Yeast type | Headspace required | Max fill |
|-----------|-------------------|---------|
| Standard (US-05, Verdant etc.) | 20% | 80% of vessel volume |
| Kveik (all strains) | 30% | 70% of vessel volume |
| 8L Oxebar under pressure | — | **5L absolute maximum** |

The 30% kveik headspace figure is a recommendation from Kveik Yeastery. Kveik produces vigorous fermentation and excessive krausen — in a pressure vessel this risks interfering with the PRV and spunding valve, which is a safety issue.

### Fermenter fill limits

| Fermenter | Standard yeast max | Kveik max |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------|
| 20L | 16L | 14L |
| 15L | 12L | 10.5L |
| 5.5L | 4.4L | 3.85L |
| 8L Oxebar (pressure) | **5L hard limit** | **5L hard limit** |

## Choosing a yeast

*To be written. Reference yeast register in BREW_OPTIONS.md.*

For house temperatures of 20–25°C (Swedish summers, year-round in heated apartments), **Lutra Kveik** (Lallemand LalBrew) is the recommended default. It ferments clean at temperatures where standard ale yeasts produce excess esters, producing a neutral profile that lets hop character dominate.
